## What v5 is

A general simulation and environment generator (not a KSSM lab). The artifact is an **Environment**. A question becomes an **Environment Blueprint** (a plan made first, hands-on by design: at least one drag or drop, every changed variable worked on a drawn object), then one writer replies in an **Environment Build Script** (reuses Kit parts when they help, writes everything from scratch when nothing fits), is checked in a real browser, and is labelled for what it survived. KSSM is the first optional curriculum pack; the top label is `source-verified`. Target: a new Environment live in about 35 s (v4 took about 4 minutes for a generated one).
